Then you paste the translation back in, and suddenly the formatting is gone. The task looks like a wall of text. Your team wastes time figuring out what's what. Multiply that by 20 tasks a week, and you've lost hours.

A Quick Tip for Better Translations

Here's something I learned the hard way: machine translation works best when the source text is clear. If your team writes tasks with ambiguous language or heavy jargon, the translation will reflect that.

So before you rely on any tool, encourage your team to write tasks clearly. Use short sentences. Avoid idioms. Be specific. The cleaner your input, the cleaner your output.

And if you need to polish a translation after it's done, you can use a paraphraser to rephrase it for better flow. I do this sometimes when the translation feels a bit stiff — just run it through the paraphraser, and it sounds more natural.
